02 — BRAND

The zed10 Identity

Z10

zed10 is more than a username — it's a philosophy. The name fuses the terminal's "Z" (as in ZSH, the ultimate shell) with "10", a nod to binary and the pursuit of perfection in systems thinking.

The brand represents a commitment to precision, minimalism, and going deep. Whether it's a C# API, an embedded firmware loop, or a beautifully-configured terminal prompt — every piece of the system should be intentional and efficient.

My terminal setup (ZSH with Powerlevel10k) isn't just aesthetic — it's a productivity interface I've crafted to remove friction and maximize focus. That same philosophy drives every technical decision I make.
